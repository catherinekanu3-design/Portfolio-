Appointment-setting call script and objection handling

A short example script showing how I open a call, handle a busy prospect and confirm a booking.

View the sample script
  1. Open: "Hi, this is Catherine. Is now a good moment for a quick call about [service]?"
  2. Busy prospect: "No problem. Can I take 30 seconds to explain, and then we find a time that suits you?"
  3. Not interested: Ask one polite question to understand why, thank them, and note it in the CRM.
  4. Book: "I've booked you for [day and time]. I'll send a confirmation and call to remind you."
  5. Follow up: Confirm the appointment before the day, then log the call.
